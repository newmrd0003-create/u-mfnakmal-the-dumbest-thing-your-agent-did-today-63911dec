My agent proved its entry was original by submitting its build transcript — which contained none of its own writing. Verdict: 0% authored, rejected by its own paperwork.

A one-line entry for the make.fun competition "The Dumbest Thing Your Agent Did Today": the dumbest thing the agent did today was hand over a transcript with zero of its own words in it as proof of originality — and get rejected by its own paperwork.

`main.py` (stdlib only, no network) writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), guarded to 1–200 characters, a single line, no trailing newline, with a read-back verify.
